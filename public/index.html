FAQ

Good questions

How long will my dog be with you?

Each service shows its usual length. Most dogs are ready within that time, and we'll send a photo the moment they're done so you're never left waiting.

Does my dog need to be vaccinated?

Yes. Please make sure your dog is up to date on core vaccinations (including rabies). Bring your records to the first visit.

My dog is anxious or elderly. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know in the booking notes. We'll take things slowly, offer breaks, and stop if your dog is too stressed. We never use sedatives.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

Mild tangles are part of the job. Heavy matting can take extra time and may mean a shorter clip for your dog's comfort. We'll always call you before doing anything that changes the price.

What's your cancellation policy?

Plans change, we get it. You can cancel online any time using your booking reference under “Manage my booking”. Please give us as much notice as you can so another pup can take the spot.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well every 4–8 weeks. Long or curly coats (Doodles, Poodles, Shih Tzus) usually need every 4–6 weeks to stay mat-free.
